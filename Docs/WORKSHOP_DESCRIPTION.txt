[h1]Mounted Arms[/h1]
[b]Real-world mounted weapons for your colony's defenses.[/b] From a hand-cranked Gatling gun to the Goalkeeper CIWS and the Iron Beam laser, Mounted Arms adds 17 turrets with their own research tab, a [b]Military[/b] tab in the Architect menu and vanilla-friendly balance. Add Combat Extended and your air-defense guns start shooting incoming shells out of the sky.

[h2]The arsenal[/h2]
[b]Automatic (powered)[/b]
[list]
[*]M240 turret and M134 minigun turret
[*]Mk44 Bushmaster II 30 mm autocannon
[*]B-8 rocket pod: seven-rocket 80 mm salvos
[*]Mk 15 Phalanx CIWS and Goalkeeper CIWS
[*]AN/TWQ-1 Avenger with Stinger missiles
[*]Iron Beam laser: no ammunition, but a 2000 W power draw
[*]APKWS rocket battery: long-range indirect fire, like a mortar
[/list]
[b]Manned (no power needed)[/b]
[list]
[*]Gatling gun: an early-game turret that only needs Gunsmithing
[*]M2 Browning .50 and Mk 19 grenade launcher
[*]BGM-71 TOW anti-tank launcher
[*]Bofors 40mm L/60 twin and quad mounts, ZU-23-2 twin 23 mm
[*]M252 mortar: portable, can be uninstalled and carried
[/list]

[h2]How they fight[/h2]
[list]
[*]Barrels overheat and stop firing until they cool; without CE, turrets also traverse gradually and sustained fire spreads more.
[*]Without CE, barrels and launchers are restocked with steel.
[*]Shots and muzzle flashes leave the barrel tip; rockets and missiles launch with backblast and motor ignition.
[*]Turrets may explode when damaged.
[/list]

[h2]Combat Extended (optional)[/h2]
[list]
[*]Real CE ammunition (7.62x51mm, .50 BMG, 40mm grenades, 30x173mm, BGM-71, 81mm shells and more), CE ranges and aiming.
[*]With CE's "Enable CIWS" option on, the Phalanx, Goalkeeper, Iron Beam, Bofors, ZU-23-2 and Avenger intercept incoming shells and missiles; the last three also shoot down drop pods, and the Avenger becomes pure air defense. Manned guns need an operator.
[*]Unload button to empty a turret or swap ammunition types.
[/list]
